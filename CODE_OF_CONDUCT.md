# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and do not harass anyone. Harassment includes sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/rmrashahriar). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break it repeatedly.
